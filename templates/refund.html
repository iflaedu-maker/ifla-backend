Refund & Cancellation Policy

Please read this policy carefully before enrolling in any course. All payments are final and non-refundable.

⚠️ IMPORTANT: All course fees are non-refundable. Once payment is made, no refunds will be issued under any circumstances.

1. No Refund Policy

IFLA operates under a strict no-refund policy. All course fees, enrollment charges, and related payments are final and non-refundable once the payment transaction is completed. This policy applies to:

  • Course enrollment fees
  • Tuition payments
  • Registration fees
  • Material fees
  • Certification fees
  • Any other charges associated with course enrollment

2. Cancellation Policy

While you may cancel your enrollment at any time, cancellation does not entitle you to a refund. Upon cancellation:

  • Your access to course materials and classes will be immediately revoked
  • No refund will be issued for any unused portion of the course
  • You will not be eligible for future course credits or transfers
  • Any pending payments must still be completed as per the payment schedule

3. Exceptions

There are NO exceptions to this policy. This includes, but is not limited to:

  • Technical difficulties or platform issues
  • Personal circumstances or emergencies
  • Dissatisfaction with course content or teaching methods
  • Inability to attend scheduled classes
  • Change of mind or decision to discontinue
  • Medical emergencies or health issues
  • Relocation or travel
  • Work or personal commitments
  • Course cancellation by IFLA (in which case, alternative arrangements will be made, but no refunds will be issued)

4. Payment Processing

All payments are processed securely through Razorpay. Once a payment is successfully processed:

  • The transaction is considered final and binding
  • No refund requests will be entertained
  • No chargebacks or payment reversals are permitted

5. Course Transfers

Course transfers to other students or future sessions are not permitted. Each enrollment is personal and non-transferable.

6. Dispute Resolution

If you have concerns about your enrollment or payment, please contact us at billing@ifla.in. However, please note that:

  • Contacting us does not guarantee any refund or resolution
  • All decisions regarding payments are final
  • By making a payment, you acknowledge and agree to this no-refund policy

7. Acknowledgment

By completing the payment and enrolling in any IFLA course, you acknowledge that:

  • You have read, understood, and agree to this Refund & Cancellation Policy
  • You understand that all payments are final and non-refundable
  • You accept full responsibility for your enrollment decision
  • You will not request refunds or chargebacks under any circumstances

8. Contact Information

For any questions or concerns regarding this policy, please contact us:

  • Email: billing@ifla.in
  • Phone: +91 8660846165
  • Address: 16, 7th Main Rd, PNS Layout, Subbayianiah Palyam, Kalyan Nagar, Bengaluru, Karnataka 560043, India
